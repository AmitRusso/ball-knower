HOW TO PLAY

1

Five prompts, one hole

Everyone gets the same five sports prompts each day. The hole is 500 yards long.

2

Any right answer counts

Each prompt has lots of correct answers. Type one and swing before the 45-second clock runs out.

3

Rarer flies farther

Your drive is scored by how many players gave the same answer. The fewer, the longer.
